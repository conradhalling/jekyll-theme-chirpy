I was not getting enough exercise after working at home since March 2020 because
of the COVID-19 pandemic. (I am fortunate to be able to continue working). After
getting my second SARS-CoV-2 vaccination on April 5, I started running on April
10, and I increased my runs slowly but steadily to four miles. The Boston
Marathon, which usually inspires me to run in the spring, was not held on
Patriots' Day (April 19) because of the pandemic.
