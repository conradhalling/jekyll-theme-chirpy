I continued running, with some good running in Lake Oswego, where I spent a
week so I could visit my mother. On Sunday May 9 I ran all the way around
Oswego Lake, a challenging and hilly run.

Unfortunately, I hurt my lower back on May 13, and I was unable to run until May
30, resulting in seventeen days with no running. I bought new On running shoes
and began using them on May 30. On May 31 after my run, I felt like my
hamstrings were very tight.
