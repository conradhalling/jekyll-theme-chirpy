After recovering from a mild back injury in May, I bought some new On running
shoes at [Marathon Sports](https://www.marathonsports.com/) and began running
again.

But I quickly suffered from what appeared to be very tight hamstrings, and I
stopped running for the rest of June. My notes about this problem included:

> Hamstrings and  glutes were tight again today. I can't decide if this is
> because of my workouts or because of running in the new shoes.

> Mild pain and stiffness when walking downhill.

> I am still experiencing mild pain and tightness in the back of my pelvis, I
> think where my hip sockets are. I have been trying to figure out what is
> wrong. I have experienced this since I began running in my new On running
> shoes.... I have ruled out muscle pain and ligament tightness. Perhaps I have
> bruised my pelvis, I have a stress fracture, or I have arthritis.... It is
> most noticeable when I walk downhill, but it is also noticeable when I inhale
> sharply....

Foam rolling, Pilates, and strength workouts had no effect.
