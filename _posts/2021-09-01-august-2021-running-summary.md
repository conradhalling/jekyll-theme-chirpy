I continued my slow recovery from what I believe is a stress fracture of the
pelvis. My pain was fairly mild and not disabling, and it was decreasing little
by little during the month.

I did very little walking for the first three weeks of August, after which I
increased my walking. I began taking calcium carbonate and vitamin D3
supplements, hoping that they might aid bone formation.

Late in the month I was able to wear my Merrell Alpine Sneakers, which were
causing me pelvic discomfort in July. I still experienced mild discomfort when
doing Pilates the last week of August.

I am waiting for fourteen days of no pain or discomfort before I begin running
again.
