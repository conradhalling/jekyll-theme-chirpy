On Sunday, October 3, 2021 I participated in the Portland Half Marathon. Since
I had taken fifteen weeks off over the summer because of an injury and did not
have time to train for this race, I ran miles 1, 2, 6, and 13 and walked the
rest.

On Friday, October 8, 2021 I attempted to walk the Virtual Boston Marathon, but
blisters stopped me after 13.51 miles.

Later in October, I spent several days in Oregon and did some good running
in Lake Oswego, including a hilly loop around Oswego Lake on Sunday, October 23.

On Sunday October 31, 2021 I ran the Bay Bridge Run, a 10-km race across the
Chesapeake Bay Bridge.
