In 2021 I began running consistently in April. But I hurt my back in May,
and in June I believe I experienced a stress fracture of my pelvis, which
took nearly four months to heal. I walked long miles through the summer to
stay in shape, and I began running again on September 20. I did not run for
31 weeks this year.

I made several visits to Oregon in 2021 to visit my mother, and this made it
possible for me to do some good running on the Oregon Coast and in Lake Oswego.

I participated in the following races:

- Portland Half Marathon (run and walk) October 3
- Virtual Boston Marathon (walk) October 8; I stopped after 13.51 miles because of blisters
- Bay Bridge Run 10k (Chesapeake Bay Bridge) (run) October 31
- Gobble Gobble Gobble 4-miler (Somerville) (run) November 25

I ran 201.38 miles for the year. My longest run was 11.08 miles, and my longest
week was 21.23 miles.

I plan to retire on February 28, 2022, after which I will have more time for
running.
