I retired on February 28, 2022, and this gave me free time to run regularly.
I started with very short distances, building up a quarter mile on each run
until I reached two miles. I increased my running distance to six miles by
the end of the month as I worked towards a goal of running the Brooklyn Half
Marathon on May 21.
