In June 2022 my running continued to go well.

I ran the REI Co-op 5k in Cambridge on Sunday, June 5. I ran 13 miles on June
11, when my intention was to run 15 miles, but I hit the wall. I ran four times
in Oregon during a two-week visit June 14-28 that included hot weather, dog
walking, and a lot of other daily walking. My best run in Oregon was on June 16
when I ran a hilly circuit of Oswego Lake.
