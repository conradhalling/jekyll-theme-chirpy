On Wednesday, September 27, 2023, I spent my fourth day at Nickerson State Park.
(For the third day of this tour, see
[Cape Cod 2023, Day Three]( {% link _posts/2023-09-26-cape-cod-2023-day-three.md %} )).

From 9:45 a.m. to 1:05 p.m., I explored Orleans on foot, walking 9.83 miles.

<iframe src="https://ridewithgps.com/embeds?type=trip&id=346776769&sampleGraph=true&distanceMarkers=true" style="width: 1px; min-width: 100%; height: 700px; border: none;" scrolling="no"></iframe>

Orleans has surprisingly poor pedestrian and bicycle infrastructure, making it
difficult to walk or bicycle in town.

![Poor pedestrian infrastructure along Route 6A in Orleans](poor-pedestrian-infrastructure-along-route-6a-in-orleans.png){: width="800" height="600" }_Poor pedestrian infrastructure along Route 6A in Orleans_

While walking, I came upon the
[IFAW Dolphin Rescue Center](https://www.ifaw.org/projects/dolphin-rescue-center),
which contains a cetacean intensive care unit.

![IFAW Dolphin Rescue Center](ifaw-dolphin-rescue-center.png){:
width="800" height="600" }_IFAW Dolphin Rescue Center_

I explored the 300-year-old windmill in
[Windmill Park](https://www.orleanscapecod.org/2025/05/15/jonathan-young-windmill/).

![Jonathan Young Windmill](jonathan-young-windmill.png){:
width="800" height="600" }_Jonathan Young Windmill_

Town Cove is located northeast of the rotary where State Routes 6A and 28 join
U.S. Route 6 heading north.

![Town Cove](town-cove.png){: width="800" height="600" }_Town Cove_

I returned to Nickerson State Park by walking southwest along the Cape Cod Rail
Trail.

![Cape Cod Bay](cape-cod-bay.png){: width="800" height="600" }_Cape Cod Bay_

![Cape Cod Rail Trail](cape-cod-rail-trail.png){: width="800" height="600" }_Cape Cod Rail Trail_

![Nickerson State Park entrance sign](nickerson-state-park-entrance-sign.png){: width="800" height="600" }_Nickerson State Park entrance sign_

From 2:10 p.m. to 4:15 p.m., I rode every bike trail and a few campground loops
of the park for a total of 13.09 miles. There are two burial sites in the
park.

![Crosby family burial site](crosby-burial-site.png){: width="800" height="600" }_Crosby family burial site_

![Eldridge family burial site](eldridge-burial-site.png){: width="800" height="600" }_Eldridge family burial site_

![One of the kettle ponds in Nickerson State Park](a-pond-in-nickerson-state-park.png){: width="800" height="600" }_One of the kettle ponds in Nickerson State Park_

In the evening, from 6:30 p.m. to 7:15 p.m., I walked campground loops for a
total of 2.46 miles. I compiled a list of nice campsites I could choose from
when making reservations for future visits.
