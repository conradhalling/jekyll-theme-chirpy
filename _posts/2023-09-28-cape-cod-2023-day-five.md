On Thursday, September 28, 2023, I rode on the Cape Cod Rail Trail from
Nickerson State Park north to
[Wellfleet Hollow State Campground](https://www.mass.gov/locations/wellfleet-hollow-state-campground).
(For the fourth day of this tour, see
[Cape Cod 2023, Day Four]( {% link _posts/2023-09-27-cape-cod-2023-day-four.md %} )).

On my last morning at Nickerson State Park, I enjoyed a beautiful sunrise from
my campsite overlooking Flax Pond.

![Sunrise over Flax Pond](sunrise-over-flax-pond.png){: width="800" height="600" }_Sunrise over Flax Pond_

I walked loops of Nickerson State Park from 8:15 a.m to 9:30 a.m., 3.72 miles.

I packed up and left Nickerson at 11:00 a.m., riding for 12.98 miles and ninety
minutes north on the Cape Cod Rail Trail to
[Wellfleet Hollow State Campground](https://www.mass.gov/locations/wellfleet-hollow-state-campground).
The rail trail continued past LeCount Hollow Drive to the back entrance to the
state campground, which was convenient.

<iframe src="https://ridewithgps.com/embeds?type=trip&id=198415217&sampleGraph=true&distanceMarkers=true" style="width: 1px; min-width: 100%; height: 700px; border: none;" scrolling="no"></iframe>

The ride was so short that I arrived before the 1:00 p.m. check-in time.
Since no one was in my campsite, I set up my tent and then checked in.

![My campsite at Wellfleet Hollow State Campground](my-campsite-at-wellfleet-hollow-state-campground.png){: width="800" height="600" }_My campsite at Wellfleet Hollow State Campground_

In the afternoon, I explored the area on bicycle, riding 15.21 miles from 3:20
p.m. to 5:10 p.m. I found how to cross over U.S. Route 6 on Long Pond Road.

<iframe src="https://ridewithgps.com/embeds?type=trip&id=346813547&sampleGraph=true&distanceMarkers=true" style="width: 1px; min-width: 100%; height: 700px; border: none;" scrolling="no"></iframe>

The intersection of U.S. Route 6 at Main Street in Wellfleet has no pedestrian
or bicycle access. This is unfortunately typical of roads built on the Cape.

![U.S. Route 6 at Main Street in Wellfleet](route-6-at-main-street-in-wellfleet.png){: width="800" height="600" }_U.S. Route 6 at Main Street in Wellfleet_

There were signs of widespread beach erosion at Cahoon Hollow Beach. I predict
that within a few years the Beachcomber Restaurant there will be swallowed by
the ocean.

![Cahoon Hollow Beach](cahoon-hollow-beach.png){: width="800" height="600" }_Cahoon Hollow Beach_

From 6:30 p.m. to 6:50 p.m., I walked campground loops for 0.95 miles. The park
is small, and there were maybe five other occupied campsites.
