On Friday, September 29, 2023, I checked the weather forecast, which was for
rain, so I went home early.
(For the fifth day of this tour, see
[Cape Cod 2023, Day Five]( {% link _posts/2023-09-28-cape-cod-2023-day-five.md %} )).

My original plan was that I was going to stay Friday and Saturday nights at
Wellfleet Hollow and go home on Sunday. But because of a forecast of rain for
Friday afternoon and Saturday morning, I decided to go home early. I used my
phone to change my fast ferry reservation. I packed up
quickly and departed Wellfleet Hollow State Park at 7:15 a.m.

<iframe src="https://ridewithgps.com/embeds?type=trip&id=198418867&sampleGraph=true&distanceMarkers=true" style="width: 1px; min-width: 100%; height: 700px; border: none;" scrolling="no"></iframe>

I rode up to Long Pond Road and took over Route 6 into Wellfleet. I took back roads
between Wellfleet and Truro to reach Route 6A into Provincetown. I later learned that my
route followed the [East Coast Greenway](https://greenway.org) and
[Bicycle Route 1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Claire_Saltonstall_Bikeway).
I arrived at MacMillan Pier at 9:30 a.m. after riding 21.68 miles.

I caught the 10:30 fast ferry to Boston.

From the ferry pier in the Seaport district of Boston, I rode 4.94 miles home
using bike lanes in Boston, Cambridge, and Somerville, arriving in rain at
1:00 p.m.
