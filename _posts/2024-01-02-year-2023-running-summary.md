2023 was a wildly uneven year for running. My running went well until mid-June,
when I stopped running because of pain in my left knee. I switched my exercise
focus to bicycling for the second half of the year, and I didn't resume running
until the last week of December.

I finished three races:

-   Fred Lebow Half Marathon in Central Park, New York City, on Sunday, January
    22, 2023
-   Broad Street Run (10 miles) in Philadelphia on Sunday, April 30, 2023
-   Brooklyn Half Marathon on Saturday May 20, 2023

My total mileage was 300.92 miles, only 66% of my distance for 2022. On my
biggest week, I ran 30.17 miles. My longest run was 15.05 miles on Sunday,
June 4.

I hope to resume steady running in 2024.
