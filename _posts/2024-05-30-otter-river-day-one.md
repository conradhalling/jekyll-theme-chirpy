On Thursday, May 30, 2024, I rode the first of two segments of a tour to Otter
River State Forest, where I planned to camp with fellow members of the
Appalachian Mountain Club from the Worcester area.

<iframe src="https://ridewithgps.com/embeds?type=trip&id=196980621&title=Somerville%20to%20Spacious%20Skies%20Minute%20Man%20Campground&sampleGraph=true&distanceMarkers=true" style="width: 1px; min-width: 100%; height: 700px; border: none;" scrolling="no"></iframe>

I left my house at 12:20 p.m. and arrived at
[Spacious Skies Minute Man Campground](https://spaciousskiescampgrounds.com/minute-man/)
near Littleton,
Massachusetts, at 3:50 p.m., taking
three and a half hours to ride 31.76 miles. Since I had printed cue sheets and
was careful to follow them, I didn't make any wrong turns on this trip, unlike
on my
[ride to this campground on May 19]( {% link _posts/2024-05-19-somerville-to-spacious-skies-minute-man.md %} ).
The route I had
planned was nearly identical to the Boston Spur of the Adventure Cycling
Association Atlantic Coast Bicycle Route, Map #1. Because of recent rain, I
didn't take the unpaved Reformatory Branch Trail from Bedford to Concord, riding
on Route 62 instead.

I had a nice site on the east side of the campground, #61. But at night, the
trailer across the street from my site had fairly bright LED lights on all
night, spoiling my view of the stars.

Once again, I pushed myself too hard on this ride. My riding effort was 13% Zone
1, 35% Zone 2, 43% Zone 3, and 9% Zone 4. I need to slow down to avoid
overtiring myself.
