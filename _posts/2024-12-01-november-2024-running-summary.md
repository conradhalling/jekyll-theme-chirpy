In August I stopped running because my right Achilles tendon was tight and
painful. After ten weeks of no running, I began running again this month,
starting out with short and slow distances and gradually building up. I
experienced a little tightness in my right Achilles tendon for the first three
weeks of running, but it disappeared after that.

On Thanksgiving Day morning, I ran the Gobble Gobble Gobble 4-Miler in
Somerville, Massachusetts.
