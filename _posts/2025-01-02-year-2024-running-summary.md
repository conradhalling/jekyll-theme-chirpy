I had a so-so year of running in 2024 with two long gaps of no running.

I ran well for eleven weeks in the winter, followed by thirteen weeks of no
running when I devoted my exercise time to bicycling and walking dogs.

I resumed running in June, when I began training for a half marathon in
September. I experimented with tempo runs, half mile repeats, and running in the
afternoon heat and humidity in July and August. This training was working well,
but I developed tightness and soreness in my right Achilles tendon that
prevented me from completing my long runs. I decided not to run the half
marathon race.

I resumed running in November after a ten-week break, and I finished the
year strongly with no injuries.

I ran a total of 327 miles, almost 9% more than my total distance for 2023. On
my biggest week, I ran 25 miles. My longest runs were 11 miles in August.

I finished one race, the Gobble Gobble Gobble 4-miler in Somerville,
Massachusetts, on Thanksgiving Day morning.

In 2025, I hope for fewer injuries and more consistent running.
