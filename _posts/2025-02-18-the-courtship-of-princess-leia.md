Today I finished listening to the audio book of [*The Courtship of Princess
Leia*](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/193373/the-courtship-of-princess-leia-star-wars-legends-by-dave-wolverton/)
by Dave Wolverton. I was prompted to listen to this book when in my podcast
listening I reached [Hugo Girl!
Episode 18: The Courtship of Princess Leia — Toyota
Corellia](https://hugogirl.libsyn.com/episode-18-the-courtship-of-princess-leia-toyota-corellia).

The plot is fairly trivial: Princess Leia is courted by Prince Isolder of the
Hapes Consortium and by Han Solo. Han kidnaps Leia and takes her to Dathomir so
he can woo her without interference from Isolder. Of course, things quickly get
complicated. Luke Skywalker gets involved in tracking down Han and Leia. There
are many villains and several battles with some unnecessarily gory details.
Chewbacca, C-3PO, and R2-D2 make appearances.

The book contains some surprising (and creative) scenes. Han wins the planet of
Dathomir in the Star Wars equivalent of a poker game. Han is believed to be
royalty descended from a king and is referred to occasionally as King Solo. Some
non-Jedi beings (Force witches) have more powers than Luke Skywalker. The
monkish Luke Skywalker is attracted to a woman. Princess Leia seems genuinely
attracted to Isolder, partly out of political necessity and partly because he's
a very handsome man who is genuinely caring and considerate of her feelings.
Han's wooing of Leia is somewhat indifferent.

The production of the audio book was good, with many voices, special effects
sounds from the movies, and music by John Williams.

My rating is 3/5 stars (good).
