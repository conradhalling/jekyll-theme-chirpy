I have an Excel spreadsheet in which I have recorded the audiobooks I've
listened to since May, 2008. But a spreadsheet does a poor job of handling
multiple authors for a book or multiple readings (listenings). So I created a
website to keep track of the audiobooks I've listened to. Click on
[AUDIOBOOKS](https://conradhalling.com/audiobooks/index.cgi) on the sidebar to
the left to go to the website.

As you'll see (if you're interested), I have listened to fiction and nonfiction
audiobooks from a wide variety of subjects. I have focused on a few authors of
fantasy and science fiction, including J. R. R. Tolkien, Lois McMaster Bujold,
Isaac Asimov, Martha Wells, Kim Stanley Robinson, and Charles Stross among
others.

If you're looking for something to read or listen to, take a look.
