Justin Hayward was a member of the Moody Blues from 1966 until the group
dissolved in 2018. Justin wrote many of the best songs by the Moody Blues,
including "Nights in White Satin," "Question," and "I Know You're Out There
Somewhere." The Moody Blues is one of my favorite groups, but Justin also
created a great album with John Lodge, the bass player for the Moody Blues,
called <cite>Blue Jays</cite>; and he issued several solo albums with many
outstanding songs.

Today [Rick Beato](https://www.youtube.com/@RickBeato) released on YouTube his
[interview of Justin Hayward](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mTG1XZ1zyLo).
Justin has many good stories to tell. Justin has his own [presence on
YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@justinhaywardmusic), including a series of
videos from four years ago called [Tuesday Afternoons with Justin
Hayward](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLDTUf2mQwYFbcblqSWKG39PfOw1oUyOlm).

Although Justin Hayward is now 78 years old, he is still touring, and his
performances are great.
