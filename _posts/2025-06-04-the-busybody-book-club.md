<cite>[The Busybody Book
Club](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/722238/the-busybody-book-club-by-freya-sampson/)</cite>
by Freya Sampson is categorized as a "cozy mystery" by Penguin Random House. I
picked this book from the new books for June in the
[CloudLibrary](https://www.yourcloudlibrary.com/en/home.html) app.

<cite>The Busybody Book Club</cite> is a light murder and petty crime mystery
that takes place in a small seaside village in Great Britain. I found the plot
somewhat, but not always, predictable and a little saccharine, with everything
wrapped up very neatly. For me, it was the novel equivalent of a Hallmark movie.

I listened to the audiobook. The narrator, Daphe Kouma, did a very good job, and
that saved the book for me. I recommend the book just for the narration.

My rating: Two stars, fair.
