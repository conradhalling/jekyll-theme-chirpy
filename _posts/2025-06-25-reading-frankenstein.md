I am a fan of the novels of Kim Stanley Robinson, especially Robinson's
[<cite>Mars Trilogy</cite>](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/series/ZMS/mars-trilogy/),
which I have read several times. In 2018 I discovered the
[Marooned! on Mars with Matt and Hilary](https://creators.spotify.com/pod/profile/marooned-on-mars/)
podcast and listened to the forty-some episodes as Matt Hauske and Hilary
Strang analyzed the three books. I have since continued to listen to the podcast
as Matt and Hilary have read other books by Kim Stanley Robinson.

For the sake of change, Matt and Hilary decided to read and analyze
<cite>Frankenstein</cite> by Mary Shelley, focusing on the original 1818
edition. Two podcast episodes of this new series are now available. This is
a link to the introductory episode:

<iframe src="https://creators.spotify.com/pod/show/marooned-on-mars/embed/episodes/Frankenstein-2025-e33t4d3/a-ac01ipr" height="102px" width="400px" frameborder="0" scrolling="no"></iframe>

<br>
Following Matt and Hilary's recommendation, I ordered the
[broadview editions <cite>Franksteinstein</cite>](https://broadviewpress.com/product/frankenstein-third-edition/#tab-description),
third edition, edited by D. L. Macdonald and Kathleen Scherf. According to
the cover, this edition is intended "for the general reader and student
as much as for the scholar." The edition includes nine appendices containing
associated documents and notes.

So, I need to read the first chapter of the book before listening to the
second episode of the podcast.
