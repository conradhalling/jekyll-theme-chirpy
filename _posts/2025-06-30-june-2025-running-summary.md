After boldly writing a month ago that I would attempt to run 100 miles in June,
I stopped running regularly after my run on June 6. I was experiencing pain in
my left knee after my runs, especially when climbing or descending stairs.

I don't try to run through pain, so I'm running much less frequently right now,
with only five runs of two to four miles each for the rest of June to test my
knee. Since one sign of worn-out shoes for me is minor injuries, I switched from
my old Kinvaras (with more than 400 miles on them) to new Kinvara Pros. The Pros
are much squishier, and I'm not convinced I like them, but they are reducing
stress on my knees and ankles.

My knee is feeling better, but I have been through this many times before, and I
may continue to have problems for many weeks, or the pain may go away and I'll
be able to increase my running.

I am proud of what I accomplished at an age when many of my running friends who
are my age have quit running. I ran pretty steadily for 31 weeks beginning the
first week of November 2024 with an accumulated mileage of 448 miles.

I really, *really* must not run two days in a row so I can allow for adequate
recovery. With my age come other physical limitations: My long run is limited to
about eleven miles, and my maximum weekly mileage is limited to about 25 miles.
From the table below, I see that I pushed too hard, running 30 miles the week of
May 26 with eight-mile runs two days in a row; and I ran two days in a row the
following week, after which my left knee was painful enough to make me take a
break.
