After doing a lot of bicycling in September, including a seven-night tour on
Cape Cod, on September 20 I began running again every other day, starting with
slow and easy one-mile runs. Every two runs I've increased my distance by half a
mile, and I'm now up to a two-mile run. I am being deliberately cautious to
avoid re-injuring myself.
