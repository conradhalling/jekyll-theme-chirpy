In October 2025, I continued to run every other day at most to allow for rest
and recovery. I limited my weekly increases in October to 10% more than the
previous week. I increased my long run to five miles.

After limiting myself to Zone 2 running beginning April 28, 2025, I felt I was
running too slowly and not building fitness. On October 21 I began allowing
myself to run in Zone 3. In reality, I was still running mostly in Zone 2 except
up hills.
