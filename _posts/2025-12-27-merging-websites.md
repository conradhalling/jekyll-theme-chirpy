For the past few years, I have maintained two websites, this one and one
at [sphaerula.com](https://sphaerula.com/). This site focuses on my hobbies,
whereas the site at [sphaerula.com](https://sphaerula.com/) was devoted
to posts about computer programming, statistics, data management, and science.

I have decided to merge the contents of the
[sphaerula.com](https://sphaerula.com)
site into this website to save on hosting expenses. I am in the middle of
the migration as I write this, having copied the posts over but not other
items.
