On Tuesday, February 17, 2026, I streamed a great live concert at
[Club Passim](https://www.passim.org/) by
[Biribá Union](https://www.biribaunion.com).
See Club Passim's
[publicity page](https://www.passim.org/artists/biriba-union/) for this
concert.

This is the band's self-description from their Bandcamp page:

> Borrowing its name from a Brazilian wild sugar-apple, Biribá Union features
[Mike Block](https://www.mikeblockmusic.com) (cello, vocals),
[Christylez Bacon](https://www.christylez.com/)
(beatbox, guitar, rhymes) and
[Patricia Ligia](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UC9ojLzB67Z0ZZfgkrkodXmA)
(electric bass, vocals). The trio draws on the freestyle
theatrics of hip-hop and Go-go music; the effortless beauty of Brazilian forró
and choro; the earthiness of American roots music, and the improvisational
spontaneity of jazz.

Biribá Union has announced their new album,
<cite>[Pompano](https://biribaunion.bandcamp.com/album/pompano)</cite>,
but so far only the title track has been released.

At the beginning of the set, Mike Block announced that
[Claire Davis](https://bmsmusic.org/faculty/claire-davis/) was
substituting on bass for Patricia Ligia, who was on tour elsewhere (success
is good). One of the tunes the band played was based on the bourrées in
Johann Sebastian Bach's Cello Suite No. 3 in C major. I am waiting for the
release of the full album to find this track since recently I have been
studying and listening to Bach's cello suites.

Biribá Union is one of several groups that
[Mike Block](https://www.mikeblockmusic.com), a prolific and
creative cellist, is a member of.
