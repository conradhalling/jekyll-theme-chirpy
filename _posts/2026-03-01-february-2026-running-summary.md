25-26. We had minor snowfalls early in the month followed by about fifteen
inches of snow February 22-23, which wiped out running for me on February 22-28.
I got a lot of exercise shoveling snow.

I increased my runs to five miles when it wasn't too cold or icy. I'm behind
on my goal of averaging ten miles per week.
