I discovered Kenneth Pararo's [Book of Moron](https://bookofmoron.podbean.com)
podcast in the late fall of 2025 when I was looking for bicycle touring and
bikepacking podcasts. At the time, Kenneth was posting a series named "Long
Errand" about a bicycle tour across the southern United States.

This year Kenneth is hiking the Appalachian Trail, and he has started a new
series of episodes named "Walden 11." *Moron* is Kenneth's trail name. Moron
started his hike about March 6, a pretty early start date, after backpacking
four days from his parents' house to the
[AT Approach Trail](https://www.atlantatrails.com/hiking-trails/at-approach-trail-hiking-amicalola-falls-to-springer-mountain/),
which starts at Amicalola Falls in Georgia.

I am going along vicariously on this adventure. I think Moron and I are very
different people, so I'm listening partly to understand how someone different
from me thinks about and reacts to things. I did some backpacking in the Pacific
Northwest in the 1970s, including short sections of the Pacific Crest Trail, but
I have not done a through hike of any major trail. I have read books by people
who have hiked the Appalachian Trail or the Pacific Crest Trail, and I'm
interested in learning what Moron's experience is like.
