On Friday, March 13, 2026, I streamed an excellent live set of traditional and
updated Irish music at [Club Passim](https://www.passim.org/) by Riko Matsuoka,
Shannon and Matt Heaton, and O'Jizo.

## Riko Matsuoka

[Riko Matsuoka](https://rikomatsuoka.wordpress.com/), who is from Japan but
now lives in the Boston area, plays Celtic harp. Her YouTube channel
is [@fruitytomato25](https://www.youtube.com/@fruitytomato25).

## Shannon and Matt Heaton

Shannon and Matt Heaton play traditional Irish tunes on flute and guitar.
See their websites [Shannon Heaton Music](https://shannonheatonmusic.com/)
and [Shannon and Matt Heaton](https://mattandshannonheaton.com/) and their
YouTube channel
[@ShannonHeatonMusic](https://www.youtube.com/@ShannonHeatonMusic).

Shannon
and Matt create a tune of the month video series.

Matt Heaton produces a
"Backer Corner" video series that teaches guitar accompaniment. I am slowly
working through these instructional videos as I teach myself guitar.

And Shannon Heaton creates an excellent podcast,
[Irish Music Stories](https://shannonheatonmusic.com/ims/).

## O'Jizo

[O'Jizo](https://www.ojizo.org/) is a group of three Japanese musicians
who play traditional and updated Irish music. See their YouTube channel
[@Ojizo_Japan](https://www.youtube.com/@OJizo_Japan).

[Amelia Mason](https://www.wbur.org/inside/staff/amelia-mason) at WBUR
published an excellent and informative article about
[O'Jizo](https://www.wbur.org/news/2026/03/11/ojizo-irish-band-japan)
before their tour in New England.
