While I am waiting for my cello to be set up for a beginner by my very busy
luthier, Eva Broadway at
[Luthier Collective](https://www.evabroadwaystrings.com)
in Somerville, Massachusetts, I have begun searching for videos of luthiers
working on cellos.

This morning I discovered a wonderful
[YouTube series](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLy6vkduAqRo8szQcI6IA2m6N3Ss88FYKt)
by
[Kimon Argyropoulos](https://www.maestrokimon.com/),
in which he demonstrates
how he repairs violins, violas, and cellos. I sat for three and
half hours this morning watching the entire series of thirty-five videos.

Imagine the worst thing that can happen to your cello, and Kimon has probably
seen and fixed it. The eleven videos devoted to repairing cellos are:
