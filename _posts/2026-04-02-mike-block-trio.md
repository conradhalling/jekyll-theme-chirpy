Tonight (April 2, 2026) I streamed a performance by the
[Mike Block Trio](https://www.mikeblocktrio.com)
at [Club Passim](https://www.passim.org/live-music/events/mike-block-trio/).

The ensemble is led by
[Mike Block](https://www.mikeblockmusic.com)
on cello and vocals and includes
[Joe K. Walsh](http://joekwalsh.com)
on mandolin and vocals and
[Zachariah Hickman](https://www.zachariahhickman.com)
playing the bass and singing.
Tonight
[Joe F. Brent](https://www.josephbrent.com) substituted more than capably for
Joe K. Walsh.

The set included a tune that Mike said was based on the second bourée of
[J. S. Bach's Cello Suite No. 4](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cello_Suites_(Bach)#Suite_No._4_in_E%E2%99%AD_major,_BWV_1010)
and also a very nice tune by Joe F. Brent. The set finished with several tunes by
Mike Block that were inspired by Frank Lloyd Wright; these were played without
interruption, and the end result was very creative.

Unfortunately, there was a sound problem on the live feed where Mike's cello
was not miked correctly, putting it in the background behind the sound of the
double bass and the mandolin during the entire show. Club Passim also had some
internet problems, with a poor live feed that reduced image and sound quality, with
the feed dropping completely for a few minutes.

The trio's latest album is
<cite>[What Now](https://mikeblock.bandcamp.com/album/what-now)</cite>,
issued in 2023.

Mike played a Forte3D cello with the
["Wooden Old Italian Cello" top](https://shop.forte3d.com/collections/forte3d%C2%AE-cello-with-wooden-old-italian-cello-top-and-or-back).
The merchandise table included
the [Blockness Monster](https://www.mikeblockmusic.com/news/new-the-blockness-monster)
and [Cellos n' Cheese](https://www.cellostrap.com/shop/p/2-bags-of-cellos-n-cheese-pasta-and-cheese-mix).

Zachariah Hickman is playing a
[show at Club Passim](https://www.passim.org/live-music/events/zachariah-hickmans-parlor-of-playfulness/)
on April 24th.
