[Angine de Poitrine](https://anginedepoitrine.com/accueil) is a duo from
Quebec who play guitar/bass (using a double-neck instrument) and drums.
The group became famous after their performance in December, 2025, at
[Trans Musicales](https://www.lestrans.com/le-festival/) in Rennes, France,
was recorded by
[KEXP](https://kexp.org/) and distributed on YouTube. At the time of
writing, the [video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0Ssi-9wS1so)
has more than nine million views.

The guitar and bass have been modified to add extra frets, enabling
twenty-four tones in an octave instead of the Western music standard of
twelve tones. The guitarist makes good use of these microtones to create
unusual tunes that are still appealing to the ear. The guitarist also makes
use of a looping pedal. The guitarist and drummer are perfectly synchronized
in their playing, and both are excellent musicians.

Angine de Poitrine has released two albums,
[Vol. 1](https://anginedepoitrine.bandcamp.com/album/vol-1)
and [Vol. II](https://anginedepoitrine.bandcamp.com/album/vol-ii), and
the band has its own [YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@AnginePoitrine).
I like the band's music, which in my opinion is most enjoyable in performance.
