[Maple Quinn](https://www.amazon.com/stores/author/B0GK4R5YMJ)
is the pen name of a friend in Oregon whom I have known for more than sixty
years. Early in 2026, Maple began independently publishing the “Molly McBeal Cozy
Mystery Series.”

<cite>[The Moonlight Vanishing: A Kansas Small Town Cozy Mystery](https://www.amazon.com/Moonlight-Vanishing-Kansas-Small-Mystery-ebook/dp/B0GJ7B9Z47/)</cite> introduces the reader to Molly McBeal, a former resident
of Oregon who is driving across the United States to her parents' summer cottage
on Cape Cod.  Molly, who was a professional psychotherapist,
is planning to open a coffee shop in the town where the summer
cottage is located.

Before the novella starts, Molly stops in Fort Collins, Colorado,
to adopt a Mini Aussiedoodle named Star. Molly and Star form an immediate
tight bond. On their drive east, Molly and Star
stop in Sunflower Falls, Kansas, for the night. Without intending to, Molly
quickly gets involved in a search for a missing person, and she uses her
psychotherapy skills and Star's preternatural abilities to solve the mystery.

<cite>[Murder Beneath Her Studio: A Completely Absorbing Coastal Town Cozy Mystery](https://www.amazon.com/Murder-Beneath-Her-Studio-Completely-ebook/dp/B0GGXCYK75)</cite> starts with
Molly and Star's arrival in Blue Heron Bay on Cape Cod. Molly is not a stranger
in this town, having spent summers there with her parents more than twenty years
ago.

Molly's friend Joan shows her an artist’s cottage that will be listed
for sale, and Molly is interested in buying it for her coffee shop. But the
artist owner has been missing for three days, and she left a prophetic painting
in her studio that shows Molly and Star standing by the artist's dead body.
Molly and Star are soon deeply involved in the search for the missing artist and
the mystery that follows, making enemies and friends as the story proceeds.

Star is a [Mini Aussiedoodle](https://yourdogadvisor.com/mini-aussiedoodle/)
modeled after Maple's dog, Pixel. Star has a
[blue merle](https://zirathecorgi.com/2024/12/29/blue-merle-dogs/)
coat and remarkable blue eyes that range in color from azure to navy.
A Mini Aussiedoodle has parent breeds Miniature Poodle and Australian Shepherd.
Both Australian Shepherds and Poodles can carry the blue merle trait. Mini
Aussiedoodle dogs are intelligent, social, and very active, with herding
behavior and emotional sensitivity that are exhibited by Star. These dogs weigh
15 to 25 pounds and have a height of 12 to 18 inches.
