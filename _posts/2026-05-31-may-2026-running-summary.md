My running went well in May. I finished two 5k races:

-   [MOM's Run](https://www.srr.org/moms-run) on Sunday, May 10
-   [Mystic River Herring Run](https://mysticriver.org/herring-run-paddle) on Sunday, May 17

I ran [hill repeats]({% link _posts/2026-05-15-hill-repeats.md %} )
on May 13.

On the advice of a good friend, I joined the
[Somerville Road Runners](https://www.srr.org/)
and went on two runs with them, the
[Union Square Breakfast Club](https://www.srr.org/usbc) run and the
[Casey's](https://www.srr.org/thursday-night-caseys)
run.

But pain in my left knee increased, and I had to stop my most recent attempt
at running on Sunday, May 24, after only half a mile.

I had entered the
[Cambridge 5k at CambridgeSide](https://runsignup.com/Race/MA/Cambridge/Cambridge5KCambridgeSide),
which was held this morning (Sunday, May 31), but I decided to play it safe and
not run.

This knee bothers me occasionally, and I stop running for anywhere from a week
to several months until the pain goes away. In 2025, I missed about seven
weeks in the summer from this knee problem.

Since summer weather is here, I will go bicycle riding.
