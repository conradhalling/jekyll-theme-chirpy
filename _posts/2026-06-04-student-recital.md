On Wednesday, June 3, 2026, students of
[Ana Ospina](https://www.newschoolofmusic.org/faculty/ospina)
played in a cello recital at
[New School of Music](https://www.newschoolofmusic.org/)
in Cambridge, Massachusetts.

As Ana's newest student, I wasn't ready to play in this recital, but I
attended to meet her other students and learn what a student recital is like.

This was the program (30 minutes):

| Piece | Performers |
|-------|------------|
| Simple Gifts | Peter Dunn (cello)<br>Rustam Zeylanov (piano) |
| Dämerung | Peter Dunn (cello)<br>Ana Ospina (cello) |
| Lied'chen | Peter Dunn (cello)<br>Ana Ospina (cello) |
| Orientale | Terri Liu (cello)<br>Rustam Zeylanov (piano) |
| Tausend und eine Nacht | Terri Liu (cello)<br>Ana Ospina (cello) |
| Gavotte | Eli Garcia (cello)<br>Ana Ospina (cello) |
| Pavane | Terri Liu (cello)<br>Eli Garcia (cello)<br>Ana Ospina (cello) |
| Ragtime der Taucher | Terri Liu (cello)<br>Eli Garcia (cello)<br>Ana Ospina (cello) |

[Simple Gifts](https://musescore.com/user/109822777/scores/28511365) is a
traditional Shaker hymn arranged for cello.

I look forward to playing in the next recital.
