The [Stand Partners for Life](https://natesviolin.com/podcasts/) podcast
was produced by [Nathan Cole](https://natesviolin.com/) and Akiko Tarumoto,
violinists formerly with the [Los Angeles Philharmonic](laphil.com). Forty-six
episodes are available, from December 21, 2017, to December 3, 2022.

I learned about this
podcast from listening to Joel Dallow's
[The Cello Sherpa Podcast](https://www.thecellosherpa.com/).

In their podcast episodes, Nathan and Akiko talked about performing auditions,
the good and bad traits of young conductors, how an orchestral musician's skills
might decline over time, stand partner etiquette, life on tour with the
orchestra, dealing with ergonomics and injuries, and more. Episode 9 (April 25,
2018) with Nate Harrington about how a professional musician learns to integrate
with Hollywood is especially entertaining.

In 2024, Nathan Cole was named
[Concertmaster of the Boston Symphony Orchestra](https://www.bso.org/profiles/nathan-cole),
as described in articles at
[violinist.com](https://www.violinist.com/blog/laurie/20245/30001/)
and the
[Boston Globe](https://www.bostonglobe.com/2024/05/20/arts/boston-symphony-orchestra-new-concertmaster-nathan-cole/).

I am enjoying this view of the lives of professional orchestral musicians,
and I highly recommend this podcast.
