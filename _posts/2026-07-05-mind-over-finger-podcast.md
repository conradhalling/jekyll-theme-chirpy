Among the podcasts for musicians that I listen to is
[The Mind Over Finger Podcast](https://www.mindoverfinger.com/podcast)
by Dr. Renée-Paule Gauthier, a violinist and certified performance and life
coach for musicians. Dr. Gauthier started this podcast on September 7, 2018, and
she has released 265 episodes as of the writing of this post. This podcast
focuses on techniques for mindful and efficient practicing and on building a
career in music.

Dr. Gauthier asks each guest questions such as:

-   What is your story about how you became a musician?
-   How did you get where you are?
-   What are your methods for practicing deliberately and efficiently?
-   How do you prepare for an audition?
-   What is your process for preparing for a performance?
-   What does your life as a musician look like?
-   What is a performance that has stayed with you throughout the years?
-   What skills should a young musician acquire in addition to learning to play their instrument?
-   What is your favorite tool for the practice room?
-   Do you have a favorite book that you recommend to our listeners?
-   What's the best advice you've received that you'd like to pass on?
-   What's a quick actionable tip that listeners can implement today in their musical lives?
