Last night (July 20, 2026), I attended a
[Morningside Music Bridge](https://mmb.international/)
Emerging Artists Recital at the
[New England Conservatory](https://necmusic.org) in Boston, Massachusetts.
The Morningside Music Bridge is a four-week program of training and performance
for gifted musicians 12–18 years.

I attended the recital specifically to watch and listen to the three cellists on
the program, but I enjoyed all performances. These young musicians were all
excellent. Although the focus was on the young musicians, the accompanists on
piano also played beautifully and carefully, taking special care to follow the
soloists.

The recital was streamed live, and the recording is available on
[YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QA61BPLon0g). There will be
seventeen more recitals and performances over the next three weeks.
