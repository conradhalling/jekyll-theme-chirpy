[Christian Colberg](https://https://www.christiancolberg.com)
is a violist born in Puerto Rico and currently the
[Principal Viola](https://www.cincinnatisymphony.org/about/the-orchestra/christian-colberg/)
of the
[Cincinnati Symphony Orchestra](https://www.cincinnatisymphony.org/).

I learned about Christian from Joel Dallow's
[The Cello Sherpa Podcast](https://www.thecellosherpa.com/)
in the episode titled
[The Accidental Violist](https://www.buzzsprout.com/1727007/episode_players/17236966-the-accidental-violist-an-interview-with-christian-colberg-principal-violist-of-the-cincinnati-symphony-orchestra?client_source=large_player&referrer=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.buzzsprout.com%2F1727007.js%3Fiframe%3Dtrue%26page%3D1%26style%3Dwave)
and dated May 30, 2025.
Christian has a lively personality, and in the podcast episode he tells several
funny stories, including how he, as a violinist with three weeks of experience
playing the viola, passed an audition with the Baltimore Symphony for a violist
position.

Christian has written <cite>Audition Manifesto: A Guide to Taking Orchestral
Auditions</cite>; the PDF file is downloadable for no charge from a link on the
home page of his
[website](https://www.christiancolberg.com).
An unusual piece of advice is that during an audition the musician must not
deviate from the finished product created in the practice room. Christian
devotes a great deal of space to this idea.

Christian has released two albums,
<cite>[Talking to Myself](https://music.apple.com/us/artist/christian-colberg/1444546117)</cite>
(2022), and
<cite>[baylamoose](https://music.apple.com/us/album/baylamoose/1736668426)</cite>
(2024). Christian writes about these two albums on
[About the Albums](https://www.christiancolberg.com/liner-notes)
on his website.

In the podcast episode with Joel Dallow, Christian talks about how he has been
writing down musical ideas for many years, and he finally got around to
recording them, playing all of the instruments himself (except flute by Amy
Taylor) and doing all the production work. The results are very good and
interesting; the style of composition and the creativity are similar in some
ways to music created by
[Mike Block](https://www.mikeblockmusic.com),
only on viola instead of cello.

Christian also has
[his own YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@christiancolberg1563),
which includes the performance of a recent composition,
<cite>[Concertaunt](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BXxcSRSyV-Q)</cite>.
