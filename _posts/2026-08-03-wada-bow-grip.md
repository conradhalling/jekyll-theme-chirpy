I am 71 years old with mild arthritis in my hands. When I began learning cello
in May, I had difficulty establishing a comfortable bow hold. I was experiencing
a little pain in my right hand and right thumb, and my right hand got tired
after ten or fifteen minutes.

I purchased a [Wada Bow Grip](https://wadabowgrip.com) directly from the
manufacturer for $27 plus tax and shipping. The Wada Bow Grip turned out to be a
good solution for me. My bow hold is much more secure and comfortable, with no
pain in my hand or thumb. The design is good because it requires relaxing the
thumb, and it promotes proper placement and relaxation of the fingers. My cello
instructor was in complete agreement that this was a good solution for me.

After three months, my hands are stronger and I'm more confident with my bow
grip. I have begun practicing for short periods with a regular bow hold,
switching to using the Wada Bow Grip when my hand gets tired. This week I have
been able to practice for thirty minutes without needing the Wada Bow Grip.

I have to be careful to hold the bow with a relaxed thumb.
