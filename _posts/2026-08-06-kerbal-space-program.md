After NASA's recent
[Artemis II](https://www.nasa.gov/mission/artemis-ii/)
crewed spaceflight around the Moon, the old
space flight simulation game
[Kerbal Space Program](https://www.kerbalspaceprogram.com) has increased in
popularity again.

I am not really a gamer, but I'm interested in celestial navigation, orbital
mechanics, and spaceflight. I was reminded about Kerbal Space Program from
listening to an episode of Fraser Cain's
[Universe Today](https://www.universetoday.com/)
podcast, so I decided to finally buy it.

There are two versions available, the original Kerbal Space Program and the
newer Version 2. Version 2 was never finished, with many missing features and a
myriad of bugs; the recommendation is to buy Version 1. Although the software is
old, it runs fine on Apple Silicon Macs and on Windows ARM computers. Neither
version is getting updates now.

I downloaded the Steam app from
[https://store.steampowered.com/](https://store.steampowered.com/)
onto my MacBook Pro and installed it. I opened the app and created an account on
the Steam platform. I had difficulty creating a password; I used
[1Password](https://1password.com/)
to generate 20-character random passwords, but the Steam app rejected two
different passwords without explaining why. I resorted to creating a
12-character password with numerals and lowercase and uppercase letters. When I
logged in, I received an email with a five-character code to confirm my login.
So I guess security is adequate.

Using the Steam app, I purchased Kerbal Space Program and the two extensions,
"Breaking Ground" 1.7.1 and "Making History" 1.12.1, for the bargain price of
$18.13, including sales tax. I installed the software on my M4 Pro MacBook Pro
and on my Surface Laptop 7; it runs fine on both computers.

Now I need to play the game and learn how to navigate a rocket ship in space,
after which I need to explore the
[mods](https://github.com/KSP-CKAN/CKAN/wiki)
that users have created and read the
[forum](https://forum.kerbalspaceprogram.com).

There is a new, similar game under development called
[Kitten Space Agency](https://ahwoo.com/app/100000/kitten-space-agency)
that is available as a free pre-alpha release for Windows and Linux.
