[Emily Wright](https://emilywright.net)
is a professional cellist and the host of the
[Lonely Cello Podcast](https://emilywright.net/podcast/).
Emily also has a YouTube Channel,
[@EmilyWrightCello](https://www.youtube.com/@EmilyWrightCello). Emily gives
cello lessons to adult students and also teaches music theory and ear training.

Emily has an outspoken and delightful personality. She was a guest on Joel
Dallow’s
[Cello Sherpa Podcast](https://www.thecellosherpa.com/):
[“Teaching Takes Dual Personalities,” July 25, 2025](https://www.buzzsprout.com/1727007/episode_players/17557469-teaching-takes-dual-personalities-an-interview-with-cellist-emily-wright-host-of-the-lonely-cello-podcast).
Emily was also interviewed by
[Ragnhild Wesenberg](https://www.ragnhildwesenberg.com/) on her
[Musician’s Journey Podcast](https://www.ragnhildwesenberg.com/podcast):
[“Emily Wright: Cellist, Educator, T-shirt Enthusiast,” November 16, 2022, Season 2 Episode 7](https://www.ragnhildwesenberg.com/podcast/07-emily-wright-cellist-teacher-t-shirt-enthusiast).

I have begun listening to all episodes of Emily's podcast, starting with
[Episode 1 "Effortless Mastery"](https://soundcloud.com/lonelycello/episode-1-effortless-mastery),
in which Emily and [Benjamin Whitcomb](https://www.benjaminwhitcomb.com) discuss
the book <cite>[Effortless Mastery: Liberating the Master Musician Within](https://www.alfred.com/products/effortless-mastery-24-em)</cite>
by
[Kenny Werner](https://college.berklee.edu/focused/effortless-mastery-institute/kenny-werner-artistic-director-emi).
A result of my listening to this episode is that I'm now listening to Benjamin
Whitcomb's albums (which can be found on music streaming services):

-   <cite>Bach: Cello Suites 1, 3, & 5 - Gabrielli: Ricercars 1, 3, & 5</cite> (2007)
-   <cite>Bloch, Britten, Cassadó, and Hindemith: 20th Century Music for Unaccompanied Cello</cite> (2021)

In [Episode 6 “David Aks and Entry Points to
Improvisation”](https://soundcloud.com/lonelycello/episode-6-david-aks-and-entry),
Emily interviews her cellist friend [David Aks](https://www.davidaks.com). They
discuss learning how to improvise music on the cello. David talks about
learning Celtic music on the cello through attending
Alasdair Fraser's
[Valley of the Moon Scottish Fiddle School](https://valleyofthemoon.org/)
and the
[Mike Block String Camp](https://www.mikeblockstringcamp.com).
This led to a collaboration with fiddle player Joyce Pan called
[Joyce & David](https://joyceanddavid.bandcamp.com) and an album
titled <cite>new roads</cite>. Emily and David discuss improvisation as
demonstrated by
[Emily Haas](https://nataliehaas.com/),
[Mike Block](https://mikeblockmusic.com),
[Tessa Lark](https://tessalark.com/),
and the duo
[Väsen](https://vasen.se).

[Episode 8 "Shop Talk with Sandra Halleran"](https://soundcloud.com/lonelycello/episode-8-shop-talk-with)
was particularly fun to listen to for me as an
adult beginner. I learned about
<cite>[Popper 15 Easy Studies](https://www.johnsonstring.com/sheet-music/fifteen-easy-studies-opp-76a-cello-2nd-cello-ad-lib-david-popper)</cite> and
<cite>[Scaling the Tenor Clef Dragon](https://www.johnsonstring.com/sheet-music/scaling-tenor-clef-dragon-cello-wendy-bissinger-miriam-wu-nancy-liebur-boshu-press)</cite>.
I also learned that good students practice, even if it’s only for fifteen minutes a day.
