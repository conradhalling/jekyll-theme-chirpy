In April, 2026,
[Carriage House Violins](https://www.carriagehouseviolins.com)
released a
[YouTube video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gCA8Eo9v0MQ)
in which [Zlatomir Fung](https://www.zlatomirfung.com),
an artist in residence, demonstrates fundamental cello bow
strokes.

{% include embed/youtube.html id='gCA8Eo9v0MQ' %}

The strokes Zlatomir demonstrates include:

-   Legato
-   Portato
-   Détaché
-   Staccato
-   Ricochet
-   Spiccato
-   Sautillé
-   Tremolo
-   Martelé

The video provides an excellent demonstration of each of these bow strokes.
Zlatomir is an articulate speaker who teaches well.

Détaché is the only stroke I know as a beginning cellist. Zlatomir says,
"détaché is the default stroke we use when we play separate bows." My teacher,
[Ana Ospina](https://www.newschoolofmusic.org/faculty/ospina),
is introducing me to the legato stroke, about which Zlatomir says,
"legato is one of the hardest strokes to execute very well on the cello."

Zlatomir is playing many
[concerts](https://www.kirshbaumassociates.com/artist.php?aview=cal&id=zlatomirfung)
in the 2026–2027 season, and he is a member of the faculty at
[Juilliard](https://www.juilliard.edu/music/faculty/fung-zlatomir).
