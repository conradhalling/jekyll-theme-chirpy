The [Silk Road Global Musician Workshop](https://www.silkroad.org/gmw) presents
a [Performance Festival](https://www.silkroad.org/gmw-performance-festival)
comprising a faculty performance and three participant performances in a single
week of intense work. This year's workshop had about fifty participants from
around the world along with the eight faculty. After going to the
[showcase concert]( {% link _posts/2026-08-19-global-musician-workshop-2026-showcase-concert.md %} )
on Tuesday night, August 18, 2026, I attended two of the three participant
concerts on August 20 and 22, 2026. The participant concerts were held at
NEC's Plimpton Shattuck Black Box Theater, 255 St. Botolph Street, Boston,
Massachusetts, a jewel of a performance space.

All participants played in each of the participant concerts. Participants were
grouped with faculty members who helped the participants prepare a new piece for
each performance, with an emphasis on creativity and innovation. A participant
was allowed to work a different faculty member for each performance if
desired.

The faculty were:

[Mike Block](https://www.silkroad.org/artists-mike-block), GMW Boston Director, cello\
[Eddie Barbash](https://www.eddiebarbash.com), saxophone\
[Hanneke Cassel](https://www.hannekecassel.com), violin\
[Ara Dinkjian](https://www.aradinkjian.com), [oud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oud)\
[Jamey Haddad](https://www.silkroad.org/artists-jamey-haddad), percussion\
[Mei Han 韩梅](https://www.mei-han.com), [zheng](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guzheng)\
[Balla Kouyaté](https://www.silkroad.org/artists-balla-kouyate), [balafon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Balafon)\
[Veronica Robles](https://veronicarobles.com), voice

The results were astonishing. Somehow, in the course of only a few hours, the
participants, with coaching, put together new musical pieces for each concert,
demonstrating great skill and creativity. The two concerts I attended were
tremendous fun and were enthusiastically received by the audiences. The
participant concerts ended with the participants coached by Balla Koyaté, who
really got the dancing going.
