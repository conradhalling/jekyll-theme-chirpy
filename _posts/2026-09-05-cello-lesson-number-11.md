## Practice Session Notes

It was two weeks since my last lesson because I spent six days in the White
Mountains of New Hampshire.

From <cite>Suzuki Cello School Volume 1</cite>, I practiced the following:

-   "Twinkle" bowing exercises
-   "Twinkle, Twinkle, Little Star Variations"
-   "French Folk Song"
-   "Lightly Row"
-   "Song of the Wind"
-   "Go Tell Aunt Rhody"
-   "O Come, Little Children"
-   "May Song" (daily practice)
-   "Allegro"
-   "Perpetual Motion" (pizzicato and bowing)

From <cite>Blue Book of Tunes</cite>:

-   "Songs & Dances"
-   "Touchdown in D"

From <cite>Practical Method for Violincello</cite> by Schröder, I practiced:

-   Exercise 16 (notes on the A, D, and G strings)

I practiced C, G, and D major scales while watching a tuner. I played notes
and scales along with drones. I practiced the D major arpeggio, especially
since it starts "May Song."

## Lesson Notes

I had the lesson time wrong and showed up two hours early. I practiced for
a half hour and then walked around Cambridge while Ana was teaching another
student.

I had practiced "May Song" the most since my last lesson. I played "May Song" a
few times with corrections by Ana. Ana had me practice playing measures 5–7
softly (piano).

Ana had me play through "Allegro" a couple of times, demonstrating how to play
the different note types, with a ritardando and piano.

We looked at some exercises from <cite>Blue Book of Tunes</cite> that I will
work on before my next lesson.

I am still stretching my pinky on the A string when I play the D in my lessons.
I need to swing my left elbow forward when playing on the C string. Ana taught
me how to do bow pushups (flexing my fingers up and down while bowing long
notes).

It was a good lesson.

## Assignments

Practice bow pushups and playing softly.

<cite>Blue Book of Tunes</cite>:

-   "Slow Slithers," *softly*; play the melody *and* the harmony
-   "See Saw," *softly*; learn both the melody and harmony

<cite>Suzuki Cello School Volume 1</cite>:

-   Don't spend much more time on "May Song"
-   Begin learning "Allegro," following all markings (tenuto, staccato,
    ritardando, fermata, etc.)
