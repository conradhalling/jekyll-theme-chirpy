Last night (September 11, 2026), I went to a show by violinist
[Edwin Huizinga](https://www.edwinhuizinga.com)
and guitarist
[William Coulter](https://williamcoulterguitar.com)
at [Luthier Collective](https://evabroadwaystrings.com/)
here in Somerville.

Among their other activities,
[Edwin](https://www.oberlin.edu/edwin-huizinga-06)
teaches baroque violin at
[Oberlin College and Conservatory](https://www.oberlin.edu),
and
[William](https://campusdirectory.ucsc.edu/cd_detail?uid=billski)
teaches classical guitar at the
[University of California, Santa Cruz](https://www.ucsc.edu/).
Together, they call their duo
[Fire & Grace](https://fireandgracemusic.com).

I copied a publicity photo of the duo from one of their websites. Edwin has
since had his hair cut very short.

![Publicity Photo of William Coulter (Left) and Edwin Huizinga (Right)](Edwin_Huizinga_and_William_Coulter.png){:width="600" height="600" }_Publicity Photo of William Coulter (Left) and Edwin Huizinga (Right)_

The two musicians were in town for the
[Rockport Celtic Music Festival](https://rockportmusic.org/rockport-celtic-festival-26/)
at the Shalin Liu Performance Center in Rockport, Massachusetts, and they
squeezed in an extra performance at
[Luthier Collective](https://evabroadwaystrings.com/).

Through the years, the two have been adapting
[Antonio Vivaldi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antonio_Vivaldi)'s four violin
concerti,
<cite>[The Four Seasons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Four_Seasons_(Vivaldi))</cite>,
such that the orchestral score is condensed into a part for a single acoustic
guitar.

The duo opened the first half of their show with an adaptation of the prelude
from a Bach cello suite, followed by "Spring" and "Summer" from
<cite>The Four Seasons</cite>. In the second half they played "Fall," three
Bulgarian dances, and "Winter".

Albums by Edwin Huizinga and William Coulter include:

<cite>Fire & Grace</cite> (2016)\
<cite>Partita Americana</cite> (with Ashley Broder; 2019)\
<cite>Alma</cite> (2023)

During the performance, I was admiring William's beautiful guitar. According to
his
[website](https://williamcoulterguitar.com/category/william-coulter/),
William plays a Brazilian Rosewood Custom Meridian six-string acoustic
guitar by guitar maker
[Mike Baranik](https://www.baranikguitars.com/).
