Sunday, September 20, 2026, was the first of five days of camping at
[Shawme-Crowell State Forest](https://www.mass.gov/locations/shawme-crowell-state-forest)
in Sandwich, Massachusetts, on Cape Cod.

Shawme-Crowell State Forest contains 700 acres of pitch pine and scrub oak
forest with sandy soil.

## Preparations

Pedal wrench for removing pedals. Hex key for loosening the bolts that secure
the handlebar to the stem. These two tools made putting my bicycle in the
back deck of a Honda Civic hatchback much easier.

## The Drive

Routes 6A and 6 near the Sagamore Bridge were clogged when I arrived.

## Evening Camping Activities

[How to experience ‘second summer’ on Cape Cod right now, according to locals](https://www.boston.com/travel/cape-cod-travel/2026/09/18/how-to-experience-second-summer-on-cape-cod/)

I retrieved the car at 11:30. It was easy to put my bike in the back because I
had removed the pedals and turned the handlebars. I was packed by noon. I ate a
sandwich and a pear before leaving. Left at 12:30. Stopped to put air in front
right tire. Not enough to reset sensor. Heavy slow traffic on I-93, first part
of Route 3. Traffic backed up in Sandwich. Six wild turkeys on Route 6A. Arrived
at registration booth at about 2:30. Campsite set up by 3:25 in moderate rain,
62°F. The campground is mostly empty. The rain is annoying. No sidewalks on
Route 6A, may make walking impossible.

I walked in the campground and to the intersection of State Route 130 with State
Route 6A. No sidewalks, so I came back for four total walking miles. I drove to
Market Basket the hard way, going over the Sangamore Bridge twice. I bought a
turkey wrap, cookies, and five cans of Progresso soup. I ate a can of soup, the
wrap, and one cookie for dinner. An owl is hooting as it gets dark. I cleaned my
spoon and the can, brushed my teeth, set up my sleep system, and was asleep at
7:30 p.m.  I woke up at 9:15 p.m. It began raining lightly at 9:30. I was too
warm in my sleeping bag, and I took off my socks.
