Monday

I slept 11:30–5:00 with short interruptions. It rained periodically overnight, but stopped at about 7:00 a.m. I explored the campsites in the local loop. I made and ate breakfast (granola, muesli, and cocoa mix; and two cookies).

I put the pedals on my bike and straightened the handlebars. I rode all the loops in the campground, about five miles. There are some very steep (but short) hills where I used my lowest gear. My fitness is not what I wish.

It is now 9:30, and the rain has stopped for a couple of hours. The forecast predicts no rain until Friday.
