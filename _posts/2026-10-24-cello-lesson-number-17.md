## Practice Session Notes

My focused practice time this week added up to only 3 hours 55 minutes. I took
Sunday off completely; on Wednesday, Mike Block came over and helped me
install the new Block Strap on my cello, after which I spent 60 minutes
adjusting the strap and playing various pieces for experimentation.

I improved a little, but my progress has slowed down. As always, I continue
to struggle with tension.

## Lesson Notes

This lesson did not go well; I found it difficult to play accurately and with
good intonation, and I found it difficult to understand new material. While
walking home after the lesson, I realized that I had not slept well nor eaten
well before the lesson.

We played "Bow and Fiddle" and "Ode to Joy," neither of which are difficult for
me in terms of playing the notes, but I don't play these well. When we moved on
to "Etude," I made many mistakes. Ana worked patiently to teach me variations
that will help me learn and play the correct notes.

Ana introduced me to extended second position and extended first position, in
which I extend my first finger upward on the fingerboard to reach a note that is
a half tone lower (e.g., from B to B flat on the A string in first position;
from C# to C in second position). I could not play these consistently, but at
least I know what to do.
