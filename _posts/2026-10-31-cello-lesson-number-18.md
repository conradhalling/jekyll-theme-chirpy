## Lesson Notes

I slept and ate properly before this lesson, and I did much better than a week
ago.

We started with scales and scales with slurs, focusing on the two-octave C
scale. Ana had me play it multiple times, giving me pointers on how to play it
better.

We spent most of the lesson working on "The Happy Farmer." I have a lot of
trouble with bowing the slurs. Ana had me do air bowing (vertically), calling
out up or down. She also had me bow on open strings, but I had a lot of trouble
bowing on the correct open string. Finally, she had me play parts of the piece
to focus on the areas where I have difficulty. All of this is good for my brain!

Ana also showed me some finger exercises, such as touching the thumb to each
finger in order, but touching in reverse with the other hand. She also showed me
a finger exercise with pointing the index finger with the thumb vertical with
one hand while making the peace sign with the other, then switching hands. That
is impossible for me to do right now. Ana says these are good warmups for
activating the brain-finger pathways.

Ana had me work on target practice for first and second position with all of the
exercises in <cite>Position Pieces for Cello</cite>, p. 3, on the D, G, and C
strings, using the harmonic as a guide.

As always, it was a great lesson, but very challenging for me.
