I recently learned about the composer
[Steve Reich](https://stevereich.com/),
who is now ninety years old.

On October 5, 2026, I went to an
[NEC First Monday concert]( {% link _posts/2026-10-06-nec-first-monday-october-2026.md %} ),
where one of the pieces played was Steve Reich's
<cite>[Music for Pieces of Wood](https://stevereich.com/composition/music-for-pieces-of-wood/)</cite>.

I have listened to and watched performances of this piece several times. When I
first heard it, I couldn't figure out what some of the instruments were, but
watching the performances helped. (There is a single cello among the
instruments.) I would like to obtain the score, but it's out of stock at the
publisher at the time of writing.
