## Friday February 14, 2025

I am currently listening to two audiobooks: *Sodom and Gomorrah* (translated to
English) by Marcel Proust and *The Courtship of Princess Leia* by Dave
Wolverton. I am reading *Alien Oceans* by Kevin Peter Hand.

I spent the last four weeks learning how to use [Jekyll](https://jekyllrb.com/)
and the [Chirpy theme](https://github.com/cotes2020/jekyll-theme-chirpy) for
creating this blog. I wrote about the details on my other blog at
[sphaerula.com](https://sphaerula.com).

We are deep into a New England winter, with plenty of snow, ice, wind, and cold.
Surprisingly, my running is going well because the City of Somerville has done
an outstanding job of keeping the Somerville Community Path clear. I'm able to
do out and back runs up to six miles on the Community Path; for my long run, I
run out and back for six miles, take a bathroom break, and go out again for
what's left. My long run has reached eleven miles.

It's time to take my Trek Valencia bicycle to Ace Wheelworks for its annual maintenance.
It's still too cold for me to ride.
