## Friday, August 18, 2026

### Music

In
May I began taking cello lessons with
[Ana Ospina](https://www.newschoolofmusic.org/faculty/ospina)
at
[New School of Music](https://www.newschoolofmusic.org/)
in Cambridge, Massachusetts.

Since April, I have immersed myself in learning cello, reading about
playing cello, listening to podcasts about playing cello, watching YouTube
videos about playing cello, and attending concerts in which cellos are played.

The cello is extremely difficult to play, not to mention play *well*, and so far
I can do only the most simple things, none of them well. But I wanted to try
something hard, I'm enjoying myself, and I'm improving. I practice an average
of sixty minutes each day.

I have finished reading several books about the cello, including these books
which I have not reviewed yet on this site:

-   <cite>[Bach: The Cello Suites](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/bach-the-cello-suites/254D77B1F3A50AB027EA535E47D1197)</cite>, by Edward Klorman

### Running

For the first five months of 2026, I was running every other day, averaging ten
miles per week, and running a 5-km race about once a month. I started doing hill
repeats to build strength and speed. I joined the
[Somerville Road Runners](https://www.srr.org/)
on the advice of a good friend who is a member, and I went on two group runs.

Then, as happens to me once or twice a year, I began having problems with my
left knee, and I have not run since May 24. My knee is slowly improving.

I am saddened to report that two runner friends have died this year, one of
ALS, the other of a sudden cardiac incident. I miss them.
